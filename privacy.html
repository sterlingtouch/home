Privacy Policy

Information we collect

Sterling Touch collects the information you submit through our quote form, including your name, phone number, email address, service area, customer type, service request, bathroom count, urgency, and message details. We may also receive photos you send by text or email for quoting and service documentation.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to quote requests, schedule service, send reminders, provide service updates, document completed work, provide customer support, and improve operations.

SMS consent

If you check the SMS consent box on the quote form and submit your phone number, you agree to receive SMS messages from Sterling Touch about quotes, scheduling, reminders, and service updates. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share mobile opt-in data or SMS consent with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. We may share necessary information with service providers that help us operate the website, process form submissions, send messages, schedule work, or provide customer support.

Your choices

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP. You may also contact us at quotes@sterlingtouchcleaning.com.

Contact

Do not submit sensitive information through the form. For questions about your data, contact us at quotes@sterlingtouchcleaning.com.
