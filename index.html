FAQ

Common quote questions.

Do you quote from photos?

Yes. Send clear photos of the full bathroom and close-ups of buildup, stains, grout, tub, and shower glass.

Do you remove hard-water stains?

We clean many hard-water and soap-scum problems. Some permanent etching, damaged finish, or old staining may not fully disappear.

Do you handle mold remediation?

No. We clean bathroom surfaces, but we do not provide certified mold remediation, structural repair, or water-damage work.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. We bring our normal bathroom cleaning supplies, pads, gloves, and detail tools.

Do you offer recurring service?

Yes. Recurring support is available for Airbnb hosts, co-hosts, landlords, property managers, and apartment teams.

What is the minimum service charge?

Standard tub and shower reset jobs start at $149. Heavy buildup, grout work, urgent jobs, and multiple bathrooms cost more.
